# Nassau Candy Product Line Profitability & Margin Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is a business analytics dashboard developed to evaluate **product line profitability, gross margin performance, and division-wise financial efficiency** for the Nassau Candy Distributor dataset. The dashboard enables users to explore sales, profit, cost, and margin trends through interactive visualizations and filters.

The objective of this project is to identify the most profitable product categories, analyze regional performance, monitor monthly sales trends, and support data-driven business decision-making.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze total sales, gross profit, and units sold.
- Evaluate profitability across different product divisions.
- Compare regional sales and profit performance.
- Track monthly sales trends.
- Identify top-performing products based on sales and profit.
- Examine the relationship between cost and gross profit.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for business analysis.

---

## Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards**
  - Total Sales
  - Total Gross Profit
  - Total Units Sold
  - Profit Margin

- **Interactive Filters**
  - Division Filter
  - Date Range Filter
  - Product Search

- **Visualizations**
  - Sales by Division
  - Profit by Region
  - Monthly Sales Trend
  - Top Products by Profit
  - Cost vs Profit Analysis
  - Product Performance Analysis

- **Data Export**
  - Download filtered data as CSV
